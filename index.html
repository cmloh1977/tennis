iPhone tips
  • No sound? Check the silent switch on the side of your iPhone (it may mute the app) and turn the volume up.
  • Add to Home Screen for a full-screen app that works offline: tap Share (in Chrome, the share icon in the address bar), then “Add to Home Screen”.
  • Screen turning off? The app keeps it awake during a session. If it still turns off, set Settings → Display & Brightness → Auto-Lock to a longer time.
  • Sunlight: turn screen brightness all the way up.
  • Camera check: place the phone sideways, at chest height, a few metres away, with your whole body in view. Check with Camera test.
